Your first test: five copied photos.

  1. Copy five photos into a separate test folder. Keep the originals and your independent backup.
  2. In TrueArchive, choose a new, empty folder as the archive and add the test folder as a local source.
  3. Archive this source. Check the result, then open the five pictures in the destination folder.
  4. Add a second source with a few copied pictures, such as an SD card or another folder. Archive it into the same archive and check that pictures from both sources are there. Repeat the import to check that unchanged pictures are recognised.

Did that work? Tell us about your first test. Need help? Follow the setup guide.

Questions about TrueArchive

What is TrueArchive?

TrueArchive is a free Mac app that brings photos and videos from different sources into your own archive on a hard drive. It requires macOS 15 or later and supports Intel Macs and Apple Silicon. The current alpha may contain bugs. Download and alpha information.

Which photo sources can I import?

Apple Photos, SD cards, external drives and local folders, plus extracted Google Takeout exports and selected Immich uploads. Google Photos is imported from downloaded Takeout files; automatic direct Google cloud sync is not included. Set up your sources.

How are pictures stored in the archive?

In ordinary folders by year, month and day, for example 2024/2024-01/2024-01-19. XMP sidecar files store metadata alongside the media using an open standard. Existing metadata is preserved, and missing XMP files are created during archiving. Folder structure in the handbook.

Do I need a cloud subscription?

You do not need a cloud subscription for a local archive. Your archived files remain accessible without TrueArchive. If originals are still in iCloud or on an Immich server, TrueArchive needs access to that source to import them.

How can I search my photo archive?

Immich can display your archive as an external library. With face recognition enabled and people named, you can also search by person. You set up your Immich server separately. Explore the photo workflow.

Does the archive drive replace a second backup?

A single hard drive can fail. Keep an additional, independent backup. TrueArchive leaves sources unchanged during import, but the alpha does not guarantee protection from data loss. Read this before your first test.
