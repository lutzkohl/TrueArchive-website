Your pictures are everywhere.

Your holiday photos in Apple Photos. The RAWs on your camera card. Drone videos on a tiny microSD. And somewhere, there’s that USB stick from Mum.

Your old drive has folders called “Holiday”, “Holiday new” and “Pictures to sort”. The next drive holds a different selection. When you need that one photo, you no longer know where to start.

Give your pictures a place together ↓
TrueArchive for Mac

One place for all your pictures.

A drive with enough space. One folder for your archive. TrueArchive brings your pictures and videos there from Apple Photos, SD cards and selected folders – as a one-off import or on a schedule.

Your sources stay untouched. The archive belongs to you: ordinary files you can open even without TrueArchive.

Everything is organised by year and day, for example in 2026/2026-09-10. Files without a known capture date go into Unbekannt (unknown).

Originals, finished edits and available metadata stay together: capture dates, GPS coordinates, keywords and album memberships, depending on the source and format.

XMP files store this metadata using an open standard and sit alongside your pictures as companion files. TrueArchive imports and adds to existing XMP files, or creates them in the archive when none exist.

digiKam is a free, open-source photo management application for organising and searching your archived picture collection. Add your archive folder to it and search by date, keywords or location.

Once digiKam has indexed faces and you have assigned names, you can also find people. Like Mum, across pictures from all your drives.

What TrueArchive can do.

Current test version: Alpha 0.13.0.
See what has improved

Import Google Photos from Takeout
Choose an extracted Google Takeout export and archive its pictures with matching capture dates, GPS, descriptions and proven album names in XMP. Import manually; no Google sign-in or automatic cloud sync. How it works.
Recognize duplicates, keep edits
Reuse identical files from multiple sources and keep their origins in the XMP. Different Nitro recipes remain separate variants when the other metadata is compatible. How it works.
Help where you need it
“?” buttons open the relevant handbook chapter. You will also find the release notes for your version there.
Archive all sources with one click
Sync local sources first, then Apple Photos using the saved capture date range. Each source gets a result; schedules remain unchanged. Open previews and partial selections are replaced.
Import from Apple Photos
Sync the accessible library without an overall item limit. An optional saved capture date range limits both manual and scheduled syncs.
Connect cards, drives and folders
Add SD cards, external drives and any local picture folders. Each source has its own page with settings and connection status.
Safely disconnect source drives
Use the eject icon to disconnect external source volumes once work has finished. Connections stay saved for later; the archive volume is protected.
Keep pictures and metadata together
Import existing XMP companion files or create missing ones in the archive, including for RAW files. Supported metadata changes are merged; conflicts remain visible.
An archive of ordinary files
Pictures and XMP files are organized by date on your drive. Accessible in Finder and searchable with the free photo management application digiKam.
See what will change first
Check the preview for folder sources and select specific files. Your selection stays intact when you switch between sources and the destination. Your sources remain unchanged.
Keep track of your work
Follow ongoing work in the fixed status area and menu bar. Your destination folder and free space stay visible; local imports can be paused.
Regular syncs
Enable schedules for each source, for example daily. Apple Photos uses the same saved date range in small batches while TrueArchive is running and the Mac is awake.
Tag pictures locally
Apple Vision can add English keywords directly to XMP files. Recognition happens on your Mac; existing entries stay intact.
Keep the app up to date automatically
TrueArchive checks for updates daily and installs them when idle. Running and paused archive operations stay protected. You can choose to update manually instead.

You choose whether to enable schedules and keyword tagging. The metadata included and the pilot’s limitations are explained in the Handbook.

Planned modules7 plans · not yet available

These are the things we would like to add. Scope and order may change with your feedback; there are no fixed release dates yet.

Adobe Lightroom Cloud
Regularly bring pictures and available metadata from Lightroom Cloud into the archive.
Explore old picture drives
Get an overview of unfamiliar collections and choose specific folders to import. You can already import individual local folders today.
Set up digiKam
Help install and configure digiKam for the archive. Search and face recognition remain digiKam’s responsibility.
Cloud backup with Hetzner Idea
Regularly back up the archive’s pictures, videos and metadata with encryption. Upload new and changed files, keep earlier backup versions and restore the archive if a drive fails. Cloud storage is required separately.
Free up storage
A separate, optional module: later, selectively remove large videos from Apple Photos after their archiving has been verified. This is not part of the core app.
Connect Immich Idea
Later, view the archive through your own Immich server without changing the archive structure.
Create photo books Idea
Get local picture suggestions from your archive and select them by swiping or using the keyboard. Book design and handoff to a printing service are planned for later.
