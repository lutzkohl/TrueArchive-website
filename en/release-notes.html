Storage must be purchased separately and the backup password kept independently of the Mac. Scheduling remains off until explicitly enabled. Download Alpha 0.38.0.

Alpha 0.34.1 · Archive one item at a time, newest first

Build 63 · 15 September 2026 · Alpha release

Cards, drives and folders start with the newest known captures. After reading capture metadata and matching XMP sidecars, TrueArchive verifies and finishes each item separately. The first completed item no longer waits for full checksums of every other original.

Interrupting keeps completed media and XMP files, which are recognised on the next run. Date filters, original verification, duplicate detection and changed-source protection remain in place. Items with no known date come last.

Preparation shows “Reading capture dates” and progress. No extra switch or persistent media catalogue. Google Takeout/Lightroom exports and saved previews retain full preparation. Actual duration still depends on your collection and drive.

Also included since the last public release, 0.28.0:

  • Clearer sources with individual pastel surfaces, directly accessible tabs, prominent Photos permission actions and improved reconnection.
  • Schedules with a time of day, optional archiving on connection and “Stop and eject” for individual local sources.
  • More Apple Photos accounts through the small “TrueArchive Photos Access” app in each additional Mac user, without a code to remember.
  • Optional space freeing with a target, preview and protection rules; off by default, with a second backup required and favorites protected.
  • Remaining-time estimates, suggestions for missing capture locations and the optional rule “Five archive stars → an Immich heart”.

Before updating: Finish active archive work and close TrueArchive and Photos Access in other signed-in Mac users too. Update every Mac that writes to the same archive. Older apps cannot edit newer local settings; save your settings before planning a downgrade. This update does not rearrange your archive folders.

Known limitations: The alpha is not notarized by Apple. Keep an independent backup. Intel runtime, complete iCloud transfers over extended periods and acceptance with personal drives remain unverified. LRF previews are not archived. Space freeing refuses deletion if the system inspection is incomplete.

0.19.0 · Internal milestone: archive selected Immich uploads

Build 43 · Not released separately; included in 0.20.0 · 11 September 2026

This internally verified milestone added Immich as a separate source and made archiving easier while automatic keyword analysis was active. It was not released separately. The following features are included in public release 0.20.0; this 0.19.0 entry records their development stage.

  • Immich per account: Connect to Immich 3.2.0 through HTTPS with a separate API key stored in the macOS keychain. Select uploads first, then check and deliberately archive them. Other accounts and external library files are excluded. Immich needs its own selection; “Archive all sources” and schedules do not import it automatically.
  • Verified imports: Selected originals are temporarily downloaded to the Mac for the preview, with at most two downloads at once. Archive status is determined afterwards. A SHA-1 comparison, SHA-256 in the archive and renewed account, selection and destination checks protect the import. Cancellation stops transfers safely; a new attempt starts the download again.
  • Originals and metadata: Unchanged originals, Live Photo partners and available exportable XMP data are included. API metadata and provenance contribute to the shared XMP file; unknown existing fields are preserved or lead to a conflict when information contradicts. Metadata coverage remains partial. Albums and sharing settings are not migrated, and uploads are not deleted.
  • View the archive in Immich: An existing external archive library can be explicitly refreshed after a successful import. This requires a separate administrator key and confirmation of additional year paths. The scan then runs in Immich; requesting it does not confirm completion.
  • Archive during keyword analysis: An available archive action can be requested while analysis is running or paused. “Interrupt and archive” waits for analysis to stop safely and then starts the requested action. A changed selection, source or archive destination prevents it from starting.
