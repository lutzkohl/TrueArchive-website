Choose the Lexar “Magnetic Set” version. The regular SL500 does not include the magnetic mount. Attachment requires a suitable iPhone, a MagSafe-compatible case or the supplied magnetic ring. The magnet holds the SSD; the short USB-C cable carries the data. Recording ProRes directly to an SSD also requires a supported iPhone Pro model.

Archiving with TrueArchive

TrueArchive runs on Mac. Set up your archive destination as APFS or HFS+. A travel SSD can hold your archive while you are away. For copying files on iPhone or iPad, use the Files app there.

Recording or copying on iPhone

Direct ProRes recording requires exFAT. The Files app supports additional Apple-compatible formats. Later, import these files on your Mac into a TrueArchive archive on a suitable destination volume. Formatting erases existing data. Apple’s ProRes requirements · External storage in Files.

How to connect it

  1. Connect a USB-C PD charger to the hub’s PD charging input.
  2. Connect the hub’s attached cable to the USB-C port on your iPhone, iPad or Mac.
  3. Insert your SD card. Connect the SSD to the USB-C data port, not PD.
  4. On iPhone or iPad, use Files to copy photos from the card to your SSD. On Mac, TrueArchive imports them into your archive.

The hub’s USB data ports share bandwidth. Your phone, cable and drive determine actual speed. This recommendation is based on ports and manufacturer specifications, not our own hands-on test. Try the combination with your device before your trip; this setup is not intended for Lightning iPhones.

Try the whole setup before your trip.

The right version

Check capacity, model number, seller and package contents on Amazon. The Lexar should include the Magnetic Set; a desktop HDD needs the right power adapter for your sockets.

A complete trial run

Copy a test card, open a few photos from the destination and try charging while copying. Plan capacity around your actual daily storage needs.

Another copy

One SSD is still just one copy. Keep your photos on the SD cards until another verified copy exists. Store copies separately where possible.
